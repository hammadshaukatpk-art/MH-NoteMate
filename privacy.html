Privacy Policy

Notes, Tasks & Reminders in One Place

Effective date: October 16, 2026  ·  App version: 1.0 (Build 14)  ·  Android package: com.mhdevelopers.notemate

This Privacy Policy explains how MH NoteMate, an Android productivity app developed by MH Developers, handles information when you create notes, manage tasks, and schedule reminders. It applies to the app's current Version 1.0, Build 14.

Privacy at a glance: Your notes, tasks, reminder details, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. The current app does not require an account, does not request Internet access, and does not upload your note content to an MH Developers server.

1. Information stored on your device

MH NoteMate stores information you choose to enter, including note and task titles, written content, task completion status, categories, selected note colors, pinned status, reminder dates and times, advance-reminder settings, repeat schedules, and selected alarm-tone references. The app also stores local preferences, such as notification prompts, and limited technical error information for troubleshooting.

This information is stored in the app's private local storage using an SQLite database and Android preferences. No account registration is required to use the app.

2. How the app uses information

The app uses locally stored information to create, display, edit, search, organize, pin, and delete notes and tasks; show the home dashboard and calendar; track completed tasks; schedule reminders; and play or display alarms and notifications at the times you choose.

MH NoteMate supports advance reminder alerts, daily and weekly repeating reminders, selectable alarm tones, and restoration of scheduled alarms after a device restart, app update, or time-setting change. Android device settings and permissions may affect whether an alarm rings exactly on time.

3. Data collection, sharing, and online services

The current release does not request the Android Internet permission and does not contain a developer-operated cloud sync service, account system, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK. MH Developers does not receive, sell, or share your saved note and task contents through this version of the app.

Android and Google Play may independently process information related to app installation, device services, security, or diagnostics under their own terms and privacy policies. Such platform processing is separate from data collection by MH NoteMate.

4. Android permissions and device access

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to display upcoming reminders, due-time alerts, and alarm status notifications when permission is granted.
  • Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): to request timely scheduled reminders. If access is not granted, Android may delay an alarm.
  • Device restart and time changes (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED and system broadcasts): to restore scheduled reminders after a restart, app update, or clock/time-zone change.
  • Foreground service and media playback: to run the alarm-ringing service and play the selected alarm sound while a reminder is due.
  • Wake lock and vibration: to support reminder delivery and alerts when the device is idle, subject to Android settings.

The current app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or Internet connection. You can manage notification and alarm access in Android settings.

5. Alarm tones and notifications

If you select an alarm tone, the app stores a reference to the chosen tone on your device, not an uploaded copy of the audio. At reminder time, Android may display notifications and the app may play the selected or a default alarm sound. The app includes a fallback notification when the normal alarm service cannot start. Notification permissions, alarm access, sound volume, Do Not Disturb, and device battery restrictions can affect these features.

6. Local diagnostics

To help identify app errors, MH NoteMate may record limited crash or alarm error details in private local app preferences. These records are not automatically sent to MH Developers. The current release does not provide a remote diagnostic upload service.

7. Data retention and deletion

Notes and tasks remain in local app storage until you delete them within MH NoteMate or remove the app's stored data. You can delete individual notes and tasks using the app's delete controls. To remove all locally stored app information, you can clear MH NoteMate's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Important: Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app can permanently delete your notes and reminder settings. The current app does not provide cloud backup, and Android application backup is disabled for MH NoteMate. Because the app does not operate a user account or remote note database, there is no developer-held note archive to delete.

8. Security

MH NoteMate uses Android's app-private storage for saved content and preferences. We recommend using your device's screen lock and keeping Android updated. No storage system can guarantee complete security, so avoid storing information you would not want accessible to someone who can unlock your device.

9. Children's privacy

MH NoteMate is a general-purpose productivity app and is not specifically directed to children. The current app does not operate an online service that knowingly collects children's personal information.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may revise this policy when MH NoteMate's features or information-handling practices change. Updates will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date. If future versions introduce online accounts, cloud services, advertising, or analytics, the policy and applicable Google Play disclosures will be updated.

11. Contact MH Developers

For questions about this policy, contact MH Developers through the MH NoteMate GitHub support page. GitHub issues are public; please do not post private notes, phone numbers, passwords, or other sensitive information there.
