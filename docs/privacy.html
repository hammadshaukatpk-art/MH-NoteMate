MH NoteMate — Privacy Policy

MH Developers · Last updated: October 15, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the MH NoteMate Android application (com.mhdevelopers.notemate), provided by MH Developers, handles information. MH NoteMate helps you create and manage notes, tasks and reminders.

Information you enter

The app stores note titles, note contents, task details, categories, reminder dates and times, and related preferences in a local database on your device. Based on the current version of the app, this information is not sent to an MH Developers server or shared with third parties by the app.

Data collection and sharing

The current app does not include account registration, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or a cloud-sync service. MH Developers does not collect or sell your note and task contents through this app. Standard Google Play services and your device operating system may process their own diagnostic and service information under their respective privacy policies; this is separate from any collection by MH NoteMate.

Permissions and device features

  • Notifications: to display reminders you schedule. On supported Android versions, you can allow or deny notification permission.
  • Exact alarms: to schedule reminders close to your selected times, where Android permits this.
  • Boot completed / time changes: to restore upcoming reminder schedules when your device restarts or its time settings change.
  • Vibration and foreground service: to alert you when a reminder is due.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or Internet permissions in the current release.

Data storage, retention and deletion

Your notes and tasks remain on your device until you delete them in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can permanently remove your locally stored notes and tasks. The current app does not provide a remote backup or cloud account. Android's application backup is disabled for this app.

Children's privacy

MH NoteMate is a general-purpose productivity app and is not specifically designed for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated online service.

Security

App data is kept in Android's app-private storage. However, no device or software system can guarantee absolute security. Use your device's screen lock and security features to protect sensitive notes.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if app features or data practices change. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised date. If a future version introduces online services, advertising or analytics, this policy and Google Play disclosures will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact MH Developers through the project's public GitHub support and issue page. Please do not include sensitive personal information in public issues.
